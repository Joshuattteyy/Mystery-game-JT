The start :The council room

You walk into the Council Room of Olympus. The room is large, with tall marble pillars and golden torches on the walls. The Olympian gods are sitting around a long table. But one seat is empty. Apollo’s seat. You were called to Olympus because Apollo has disappeared. No one knows where he went or what happened to him. The gods have searched for him, but they haven’t found any answers. Zeus looks at you. “Apollo is missing,” he says. “We need someone to find out what happened to him.” You look around the room. Everyone seems worried, but no one seems to know anything. It is now your job to find Apollo. The investigation begins here.
